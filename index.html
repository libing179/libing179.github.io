📄 学术论文

CCF‑C NPCL: Negative‑Preserving Contrastive Learning under Noisy Correspondence [PDF]
李冰（第一作者）, Jiaqi Xue, Hongji Sun
ICPR 2026 (已录用)
  • 研究背景：互联网采集图文数据集普遍存在噪声配对问题，错误匹配图文被当作正样本训练，严重降低跨模态检索性能。（1）现有方法大多侧重过滤、降权噪声样本，忽略负样本监督作用，高噪声场景下判别能力退化严重。（2）现有方法的样本分区依赖伪标签细化或不确定性建模，本质上从正样本对出发来判断匹配可靠性。但高噪声下正样本本身已被污染，导致分区错误，且错误在后续优化中持续放大，形成恶性循环。（3）分类中的噪声标签学习不适用于检索噪声对应，因为噪声对应代表由成对关系而不是显式标签产生的实例级噪声。
  • 核心创新：提出NPCL噪声保留型对比学习框架。①多视图特征聚合双向跨模态证据提取器；②基于狄利克雷分布联合估计匹配置信度与不确定性，实现样本自动划分干净/噪声集合；③设计噪声感知增强负样本对比损失、噪声解耦对比损失，抑制噪声正样本干扰同时完整保留负样本判别信息。
  • 实验：在Flickr30K、MS‑COCO‑1K数据集，设置20%/50%/70%梯度噪声比例；相比NCR、DECL、UGNCL等SOTA方法，rSum综合检索指标持续领先，尤其70%极端噪声条件下模型性能衰减显著更小，验证方法的噪声鲁棒性。

📄 Publications

CCF‑C NPCL: Negative‑Preserving Contrastive Learning under Noisy Correspondence [PDF]
Bing Li (First Author), Jiaqi Xue, Hongji Sun
ICPR 2026 (Accepted)
  • Background: Web‑collected image‑text datasets suffer from noisy correspondence, where mismatched pairs are treated as positives and degrade retrieval performance. Existing works mainly suppress noisy samples while ignoring supervision from negative samples, leading to severe performance drop under high‑noise regimes. Sample partition methods rely on noisy positive pairs themselves, creating cascading errors. Noise‑label learning for classification cannot be directly adapted for pairwise retrieval noise.
  • Contributions: We propose NPCL, a negative‑preserving contrastive learning framework. ① Multi‑view bidirectional cross‑modal evidence extractor; ② Dirichlet‑distribution to jointly estimate matching confidence & uncertainty for clean/noisy sample partition; ③ Noise‑aware negative‑enhanced contrastive loss and noise‑decoupled contrastive loss to suppress noisy positives while preserving discriminative negative signals.
  • Experiments: Evaluated on Flickr30K, MS‑COCO‑1K under 20% / 50% /70% noise ratios. Our method outperforms SOTA approaches (NCR, DECL, UGNCL) in overall rSum metric, showing strong robustness especially under extreme 70% noise setting.
